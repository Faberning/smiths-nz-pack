SMITH'S NZ SYSTEMS PACK - HOW TO PRESENT
Prepared by Eko Media, October 2026

1. Keep all the files together in this folder. If you send it to someone as a zip, they need to "Extract All" first.
2. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge. This is the presentation. Use the arrow keys or the dots on the right to move between slides.
3. The buttons in the top bar open the live demos:
   - Installer portal (portal.html): stock and lead times, the site calculator, a quote with a back-ordered item, training and leads.
   - Network map (map.html): approved installers, demand by town and the 5 coverage gaps. Switch to "Homeowner view" to search for a town.
   - Leadership dashboard (dashboard.html): what Smith's would see each week.
   Each demo has a "Back to presentation" button that takes you back to the slide you came from.
4. The film is in the video folder:
   - smiths-promo-169.mp4 for the boardroom screen
   - smiths-promo-916.mp4 for phones
   The last slide links to both.
5. Everything works offline. No internet is needed.

All installers, stock levels, demand and dashboard figures are SAMPLE data for discussion and are labelled that way. There is no pricing in the pack.
