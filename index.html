02 · The gap

The stock, the training and the installers are there. Nothing joins them up.

We reviewed smithsnz.co.nz as an installer would. It's a good online store with a customer login. What we couldn't find were the tools an installer needs between "I've got a job" and "I've placed the order".

Every one of these questions is a sales signal. Today, Smith's only sees it when the order lands, or when someone picks up the phone.

03 · The model

One central brain, three legs

Each leg gets the tools it needs, and they all draw on the same live data, so a stock change in Hamilton shows up in every installer's quote straight away.

1Marketing

The homeowner front door for the brands Smith's carries, starting with Sungrow.

  • Homeowner cost calculator
  • Find-an-installer map
  • Local content that gets found in Google and AI answers

2Smith's operations

The single source of truth: stock, product rules and training.

  • Live stock and lead times
  • Compliance and sizing rules
  • Training library and demand reports

3Approved installers

One login that does the job from site visit to order.

  • Address-based system sizing
  • Quotes that check stock
  • Their own local microsite, owned by Smith's

04 · Integrations

Built on what Smith's already runs

No rip-and-replace. Each system Smith's uses today connects into the brain, and the brain feeds every tool. Where a system has an API we connect it directly. Where it doesn't, we use scheduled exports or AI plug-ins.

Keep what works

The store, the accounts and the warehouse stay as they are. The platform reads from them and writes back only where Smith's approves it.

One source of truth

Stock, product rules and training live in one place, so the portal, the calculator and the dashboard never disagree.

AI with a human in the loop

One main agent is allowed to make changes. Other agents can read and suggest, and every change needs a person's approval.

These systems are examples until Smith's confirms what it runs.

05 · Leg 3 · Installer portal

From site visit to order in one login

Every approved installer gets a portal that knows Smith's stock and the rules for each address. It's quicker for the installer and gives Smith's a clear view of the pipeline.

⌂

Address calculator

Enter a site address. It looks up the wind zone, coastal exposure, flood overlays and council notes, and shows only products that comply.

▦

Live stock and lead times

Shows what's on the shelf in Hamilton, what's inbound and when, and the nearest alternative if something is out.

$

Quotes that check stock

Flags low or back-ordered stock before the installer promises a date, and recommends available products.

▶

Training on the job

"How do I install this racking system?" opens the right video and the install manual, right inside the quote.

!

Notifications

Stock arrivals, price changes, recalls and new products, pushed to the installers they affect.

Demand signals

Every lookup and quote is logged, so Smith's sees demand by product and region before the orders come in.

06 · Leg 1 · Homeowner front door

Homeowners price it, Smith's installers win it

A public calculator takes an address and a power bill. It estimates the right system size, then sends the homeowner to the nearest approved installer as a ready-made lead.

The same map works the other way for Smith's. It shows where the network is thin and where to recruit next.

07 · The asset

Smith's owns the network's digital footprint

Each approved installer gets a local microsite with its own content and Google Business Profile, all linking back to the main site. The installer runs the jobs, and Smith's owns the sites.

If an installer leaves

The microsite, its search ranking, its reviews and its leads stay with Smith's and are handed to the next approved installer in that area.
